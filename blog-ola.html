Ola and Its Visionary CEO Bhavish Aggarwal: Revolutionizing Mobility in India

E
E-Cell Team
• October 7, 2025 • visibility 523 views

Ola, formerly known as Ola Cabs, is India's largest mobility platform and one of the world's leading ride-hailing companies. Founded in 2010, Ola has transformed urban transportation across India and several other countries. At the heart of this transformational journey is Bhavish Aggarwal, the co-founder and CEO, whose vision to redefine mobility has made Ola a household name.

The Origin and Growth of Ola

Ola was founded in December 2010 by Bhavish Aggarwal and Ankit Bhati, two graduates of the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Mumbai. The idea was born out of Bhavish's frustrating experience of being stranded during a taxi trip when the driver demanded more money and abandoned him midway. This incident revealed the chaotic and unreliable state of taxi services in India. Bhavish saw an opportunity to create a reliable, tech-driven cab service that would revolutionize urban mobility.

Starting as a taxi aggregation platform, Ola allowed customers to book rides via phone calls and later through a mobile app launched in 2012. From a modest beginning in Mumbai, Ola rapidly expanded its service to over 250 cities in India and ventured internationally to Australia, New Zealand, and the UK before consolidating its focus on the Indian market.

Ola diversified beyond cab services into various mobility solutions such as autos, bikes, electric vehicles, and even public transport ticketing through its acquisition of Ridlr. It also expanded into adjacent businesses like cloud kitchens, financial services, and fleet management under Ola Consumer.

Bhavish Aggarwal: The Man Behind Ola

Bhavish Aggarwal is a technology enthusiast and entrepreneur who graduated from IIT Bombay with a degree in Computer Science. Before founding Ola, he worked at Microsoft Research where he co-authored papers and secured patents, gaining valuable experience in technology development.

Driven by the mission to build mobility for a billion people, Bhavish has maintained a hands-on approach, even driving cabs himself in the early days to understand both driver and customer challenges first-hand. He is known for his belief in customer-centric innovation and a vision to make transportation affordable, accessible, and convenient for millions.

Bhavish is also noted for his relatively low media profile despite Ola's massive scale, focusing instead on product innovation and sustainable growth. As CEO, he has steered Ola through competitive pressures and evolving market dynamics, emphasizing profitability and operational efficiency in recent years.

Ola's Journey and Impact

Ola holds a dominant market share in India, competing closely with global peer Uber. The company's technology platform connects over 1.5 million driver-partners to hundreds of millions of consumers. Ola's innovations include Ola Play (an in-cab infotainment system), Ola Electric (India's largest electric vehicle manufacturer), and Ola Fleet Technologies (fleet management solutions).

The company has also shown resilience amid market challenges, achieving EBITDA profitability in FY24 and narrowing its losses significantly. Recently rebranded as Ola Consumer, the company is focusing on integrated, sustainable growth and expanding its ecosystem beyond mobility into areas like micro-insurance and consumer credit.

Conclusion

Bhavish Aggarwal's leadership has been instrumental in Ola's rise as India's premier ride-hailing giant and a prominent global mobility player. From a frustrating taxi ride experience to spearheading one of the country's most successful startups, Aggarwal's journey symbolizes innovation, grit, and a commitment to improving millions of lives through technology.

Ola's story underlines the potential of homegrown solutions to solve uniquely Indian problems, creating scalable impact through relentless customer focus and technology-driven disruption. As Bhavish and his team continue to innovate, Ola looks set to remain central to the future of mobility in India and beyond.
