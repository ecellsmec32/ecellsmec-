About E-Cell

The Entrepreneurship Cell (E-Cell) at St. Martin's Engineering College is dedicated to fostering a culture of innovation and entrepreneurship among students. Our mission is to provide resources, mentorship, and opportunities for aspiring entrepreneurs to develop their ideas and launch successful ventures. We envision a future where every student with a passion for entrepreneurship has the support and guidance they need to thrive.
